TaxReceiptSyncr

TaxReceiptSyncr is a small personal tool that automatically files business receipts and invoices into the right Google Drive tax-year folder.

Receipts arrive in the author's own Gmail account (a Bunnings invoice as a PDF attachment, or an Australia Post receipt link forwarded from an SMS). A scheduled job saves each receipt as a PDF in the author's own Google Drive, in a folder named for the Australian financial year (for example 2026 - 2027), and then labels the email so it is not processed twice.

Who it is for

It is built for and used only by its author, Glenn Reilly, on his own Google account. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not collect data from anyone else.

Google permissions

To do this, the app asks for access to the author's Gmail (read messages, apply labels, send one summary email to himself) and Google Drive (find and create folders and files). The privacy policy explains exactly how that access is used.
